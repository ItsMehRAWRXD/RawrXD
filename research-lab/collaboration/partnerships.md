## Partnership Framework

### Types of Agreements

#### 1. Research Agreement
- Joint research projects
- Shared resources
- Co-authored publications
- IP sharing terms

#### 2. Sponsored Research
- Funding for specific projects
- Milestone-based payments
- Deliverable requirements
- Reporting obligations

#### 3. Data Sharing Agreement
- Dataset access
- Privacy protections
- Usage restrictions
- Attribution requirements

#### 4. Technical Collaboration
- Code contributions
- Review processes
- Integration support
- Documentation sharing

### Agreement Structure

```
1. Purpose & Scope
2. Term & Termination
3. Resources & Responsibilities
4. Intellectual Property
5. Publications & Publicity
6. Confidentiality
7. Liability & Indemnification
8. Dispute Resolution
9. General Provisions
```

## How to Partner

### For Academic Institutions

1. **Initial Contact**
   - Email: research@rawrxd.local
   - Include: Research interests, proposed collaboration

2. **Exploration Phase**
   - Video call to discuss alignment
   - Review mutual capabilities
   - Identify specific projects

3. **Agreement Development**
   - Draft research agreement
   - Legal review (both parties)
   - Sign and execute

4. **Project Launch**
   - Kickoff meeting
   - Resource allocation
   - Timeline establishment
   - Regular sync meetings

### For Industry Partners

1. **Initial Inquiry**
   - Email: partnerships@rawrxd.local
   - Include: Company profile, use case, goals

2. **Discovery Call**
   - Understand requirements
   - Present capabilities
   - Explore collaboration models

3. **Proposal Development**
   - Scope definition
   - Resource planning
   - Timeline creation
   - Budget estimation

4. **Agreement Execution**
   - Contract negotiation
   - Legal review
   - Signature and kickoff
