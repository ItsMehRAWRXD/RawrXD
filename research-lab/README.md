# RawrXD Research Lab (RRL)

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Established:** July 13, 2026  
**Mission:** Continuous innovation in sovereign AI inference

---

## Overview

The RawrXD Research Lab (RRL) is the dedicated research division for ongoing innovation in sovereign AI inference technology. RRL drives the roadmap from v1.1.0 through v2.0.0 and beyond.

---

## Research Areas

### Active Research Streams

1. **Vision Models** (v1.1.0)
   - CLIP integration
   - LLaVA multimodal support
   - Image tokenization

2. **Function Calling** (v1.1.0)
   - Structured output generation
   - Tool use capabilities
   - API orchestration

3. **Distributed Inference** (v1.2.0)
   - Multi-node scaling
   - Pipeline parallelism
   - Model sharding

4. **Next-Gen Architecture** (v2.0.0)
   - Hardware-specific optimizations
   - Advanced quantization
   - Safety frameworks
