## Disposition

The native REAP Q3_K codec emits the standard 110-byte block layout, decodes
reference GGML blocks, and produces blocks accepted by the GGML decoder with
matching reconstructed values. Physical exporter selection and real-model
semantic retention remain separate open gates.
