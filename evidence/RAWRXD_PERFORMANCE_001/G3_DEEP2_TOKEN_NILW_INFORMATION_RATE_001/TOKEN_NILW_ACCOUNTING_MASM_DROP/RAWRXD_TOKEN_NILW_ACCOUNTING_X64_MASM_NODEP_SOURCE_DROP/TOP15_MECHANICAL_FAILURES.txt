TOKEN NILW — TOP 15 FAIL-CLOSED CONDITIONS

01 TOTAL_WEIGHTS_ZERO
   A whole-model denominator of zero invalidates every NILW ratio.
02 ACTIVE_WEIGHTS_ZERO
   Active BPW cannot be derived without an active subset.
03 ACTIVE_GT_TOTAL
   Physicalization may not exceed the logical model.
04 CODEC_BITS_ZERO
   The new-information fraction requires a codec-bit denominator.
05 NEW_CODEC_GT_CODEC
   New active information cannot exceed the represented active payload.
06 PAYLOAD_SUM_OVERFLOW
   new_codec_bits + overhead_bits must fit exactly in 64 bits.
07 IDENTITY_MISMATCH
   Measured transport bits must equal codec-new bits plus overhead bits.
08 NILW_TARGET_ZERO
   Both numerator and denominator of the configured limit must be nonzero.
09 PAYLOAD_CAP_ZERO
   The per-token byte envelope must be explicit.
10 RATE_CAP_ZERO
   The information-rate ceiling must be explicit.
11 TPS_TARGET_ZERO
   The token-rate target must be explicit.
12 EMPTY_RUN_WINDOW
   Minimum tokens, generated tokens, and wall time must all be nonzero.
13 BOOLEAN_OUT_OF_RANGE
   Authority and state facts are strictly 0 or 1.
14 RUN_PAYLOAD_TOO_SMALL
   Aggregate run bytes must include at least the current token payload.
15 AUTHORITY_OR_SUSTAINABILITY_MISSING
   Any missing gate keeps RUNTIME_AUTHORIZED clear; no partial pass promotes.

All ratio and throughput comparisons use unsigned 128-bit cross-products.
No floating-point rounding can turn a failing boundary into a pass.
