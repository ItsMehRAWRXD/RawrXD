POST-PRODUCT ENTERPRISE TOP 15

This overlay is additive. It does not redefine:
  residency / Morning Grouch
  provider policy
  GPU HOT semantics
  scheduler authority
  decode authority
  token authority
  PROMOTE

Hard prerequisite:
  product binary loaded
  product entry reached
  real model bytes observed
  real tensor math observed
  real logits observed
  token commit observed
  decode commit observed
  product runtime rc == 0

If the product prerequisite is not true, enterprise evaluation stops with ENT_E_PRODUCT.

01 BUILD_IDENTITY_001
   Build ID + source revision + binary/source revision match.

02 ARTIFACT_INTEGRITY_001
   Binary/package hash + manifest hash + zero tamper events.

03 CONFIG_SEAL_001
   Known schema, zero unknown keys, zero post-start config mutation.

04 COMPATIBILITY_MATRIX_001
   CPU/OS/GPU-path/model-format/codec compatibility all observed.

05 MODEL_TRUST_VALIDATION_001
   Zero parser-bounds, invalid-shard, metadata-overflow, path-escape faults.

06 SESSION_ISOLATION_001
   Zero cross-session state/KV contamination and zero cancellation leaks.

07 RESOURCE_QUOTAS_001
   Zero host/GPU/KV budget violations and zero file-handle leaks.

08 FAULT_CONTAINMENT_001
   Zero process corruption, double-free, UAF, stale-generation faults.

09 RECOVERY_REUSE_001
   Every recovery attempt succeeds; zero unrecovered faults;
   if recovery occurred, runtime is reusable afterwards.

10 AUDIT_JOURNAL_001
   Nonzero observed events, zero sequence gaps, duplicate IDs, unattributed events.

11 TELEMETRY_RECONCILIATION_001
   Nonzero telemetry, zero counter mismatches and zero dropped critical events.

12 SHUTDOWN_RESTART_001
   Zero dirty shutdowns/corruption and at least one successful restart proof.

13 ROLLBACK_001
   Rollback image exists; attempts==successes; zero rollback data-loss faults.

14 SOAK_CHAOS_001
   Observed soak minutes >= configured requirement;
   chaos cases executed >= required; zero uncontained chaos failures.

15 ENTERPRISE_RELEASE_001
   Clean-machine build/run, upgrade, downgrade, packaging and version surface PASS.

Final invariant:
  ENTERPRISE_READY=1 only when PRODUCT_PREREQ=1 and all 15 enterprise gates PASS.

No source-presence-only gate can mint enterprise readiness.
No enterprise gate can mint product/decode/token/promotion authority.
