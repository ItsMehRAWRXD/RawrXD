PURPOSE
  Minimize same-token dual-GPU critical path by matching lane finish times.
  Initial policy seeds the measured direction at GPU0/GPU1 ~= 80/20, but the
  source does not treat 80/20 as truth. It converges from measured lane times.

IMPORTANT ARITHMETIC
  If GPU1 is exactly 2.0x slower PER ROW and both lanes are perfectly linear
  with equal fixed overhead, the matched-duration split is about 66.7/33.3,
  not 80/20.  An 80/20 optimum means the effective end-to-end ratio is nearer
  4:1 OR fixed launch/dequant/tail overhead makes GPU1 more expensive at the
  current row count. Therefore lane duration and critical_ns are authoritative.

TOP_15_MECHANICS
  01  80/20 seed for the current measured direction.
  02  Same-token lane-duration feedback, not nominal bandwidth.
  03  Effective ns/row measurement for both GPUs.
  04  Q16 integer EWMA; no floating point required.
  05  Single-sample outlier clamp to 0.5x..2.0x prior EWMA.
  06  Finish-skew deadband to prevent split chatter.
  07  Skew-proportional bounded step size.
  08  Alignment-aware row assignment for packed/tiled kernels.
  09  Minimum/maximum GPU1 share clamps.
  10  Best-critical-path split retention.
  11  >5% critical-path regression pullback toward best split.
  12  Model-based settling only after durations are already near matched.
  13  Fail-closed evidence gate: timing + parity + device health required.
  14  Small-work fallback to one lane when a legal dual split is impossible.
  15  Caller-owned state, zero allocation, zero CRT, zero OS calls.

AUTHORITY
  SAMPLE_VALID alone is insufficient. D2LbObserve adapts only when:
    SAMPLE_VALID | SAMPLE_PARITY_OK | SAMPLE_DEVICE_OK
  are all present.

RECOMMENDED PRODUCT METRICS
  FINISH_SKEW_NS = abs(duration0_ns-duration1_ns)
  CRITICAL_NS    = actual same-token critical path
  OVERLAP_NS     = measured simultaneous execution window
