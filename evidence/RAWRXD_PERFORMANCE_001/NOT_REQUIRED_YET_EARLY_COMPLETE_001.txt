  Anything marked NOT_REQUIRED_YET gets a special moment to get done early.
  That moment is explicit, optional, and separate from Mode D debt and from
  HOT_GATE measurement spend.

LAW=
  NOT_REQUIRED_YET ≠ forever-forbidden
  NOT_REQUIRED_YET ≠ current Mode D incompleteness
  NOT_REQUIRED_YET ≠ automatic authorization to execute now

  SPECIAL_MOMENT =
    an explicit early-complete authorization for a named NOT_REQUIRED_YET item
    issued in-chat or by sealed receipt line

  EARLY_COMPLETE (when SPECIAL_MOMENT issued) may do:
    site-local wiring
    caller-owned EvidenceState / Init512
    CMake product link of Core+Emit
    SOURCE→BUILD of the instrumented binary

  EARLY_COMPLETE does NOT by itself authorize:
    HOT_GATE remasure
    PATH_B_N5 trial spend
    MEASUREMENT receipt as product PASS/FAIL
    claim promotion without that claim's own Commit record from a measured run
    backfill of PATH_B_N5_003

  After EARLY_COMPLETE BUILD:
    source existence still ≠ runtime evidence
    runtime claims remain unobserved until a later measured run fires emitters
    MEASUREMENT still requires MODE=A HOT_GATE=1 (or other catalog spend line)
