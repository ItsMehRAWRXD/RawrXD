MORNING_GROUCH residency ownership law (intrinsic, not loader strategy)
======================================================================

RESIDENCY_STATE_OWNS_MORNING_GROUCH=1
PROVIDER_OWNS_MORNING_GROUCH=0
LOADER_OWNS_MORNING_GROUCH=0

FIRST_ABSENT_CURRENT_OP_TOUCH
  → ATOMIC_MG_CLAIM
  → EXACTLY_ONE_PHYSICALIZATION_ATTEMPT
SUCCESS → WARM
FAILURE → NOT_WARM (COLD or FAILED)

REUSE_WHILE_RESIDENT → MG_DELTA=0 AND PHYSICAL_READ_DELTA=0
EVICTION → NEW_RESIDENCY_GENERATION_REQUIRED
SPECULATIVE_REQUEST → REJECT_BEFORE_MG_CLAIM

STATES:
  COLD | MG_IN_PROGRESS | WARM | HOT | FAILED

WARM guarantees: valid bytes, CPU ptr, matching generation, pin-owned
HOT guarantees: device id + allocation generation + source residency generation
  (device ABI optional; mark-only if no device attached)

JOIN: losers wait; DUPLICATE_FIRST_TOUCH=0; one provider read
ALIAS: full containment may hit parent; partial overlap is not a hit
HOT_UPLOAD: separate allocation copy; reset invalidates handle
  HOST_DEVICE_BACKEND = VirtualAlloc reference (same HOT/gen/pin/inval law)
  GPU_DEVICE_BACKEND  = D3D12 alloc+copy+fence only
  GPU_BACKEND_MAY_MATERIALIZE_HOT=1
  GPU_BACKEND_MAY_DEFINE_HOT_SEMANTICS=0

THESE THREE EXTEND CONCURRENCY / ALIAS / DEVICE AROUND THE FROZEN MG MACHINE.
THEY DO NOT REDEFINE MORNING_GROUCH.
