# Deep2 Link-15 Law

- TARGET64 correctness evidence remains immutable input.
- Baseline full-model TPS authority is measured before optimization links are credited.
- A source landing is not a product bind.
- A product bind is not a PASS until the real callback reports success.
- No link may satisfy prerequisites by setting its own prerequisite bits.
- No source-only helper may mint promotion authority.
- Real dual-GPU means non-zero useful arithmetic on both devices for the production decode path; capacity-only placement does not satisfy it.
- 64 GB bounded streaming must be proven with bounded residency/arena behavior, not inferred from model size.
- Sweep/speculative verification remains an additive optimization after baseline authority and is not used to reinterpret TARGET64.
