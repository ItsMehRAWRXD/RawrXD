RAWRXD 671B STREAM PHYSICALIZATION — DEEP2 INTEGRATION

1. Build r671_stream.c into the Deep2 runtime.
2. Create one r671_ctx per GGUF/shard backing file.
3. Open with a fixed slot_count and window_bytes.
4. Keep tensor metadata as {file_offset, byte_length}; do not copy entire tensors.
5. Before a kernel/CPU primitive consumes a tensor slice:
       r671_tensor_acquire(...)
6. Consume lease.ptr for exactly lease.bytes.
7. After the consuming primitive is complete:
       r671_release(...)
8. Preserve leases across repeated operations only when reuse is intended.
9. Read r671_stats into NILW/runtime receipts:
       newly_mapped_bytes
       reused_bytes
       cache_hits
       cache_misses
       evictions
       mapped_bytes_peak
10. A failed acquire is a hard runtime fault; never silently materialize a whole tensor.

Suggested first 671B ladder:
    G0: 384 GiB sparse backing probe
    G1: real GGUF metadata + 1 tensor slice
    G2: one real MoE expert tensor slice
    G3: 8 routed expert slices
    G4: one real transformer layer forward
    G5: one complete token forward
    G6: 8 tokens
    G7: 128 tokens + residency/NILW receipt
