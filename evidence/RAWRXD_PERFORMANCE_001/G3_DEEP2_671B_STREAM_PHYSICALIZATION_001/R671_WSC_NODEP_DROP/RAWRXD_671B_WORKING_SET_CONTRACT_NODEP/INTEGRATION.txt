INTEGRATION WITH RAWRXD_671B_STREAM_PHYSICALIZATION_NODEP

At runtime, populate wsc15_raw directly from the byte-stream layer counters.

Per acquire:
  request_observed=1
  request_offset/request_bytes
  current_mapped_bytes
  peak_mapped_bytes
  cache_hits/cache_misses
  reused_bytes/newly_mapped_bytes
  evictions
  evictions_while_pinned
  non_lru_evictions
  leases_acquired

Per release:
  leases_released
  invalid_release_count

At token begin:
  token_observed=1
  token_id
  token_begin_mapped_bytes

During token:
  token_peak_mapped_bytes
  token_newly_mapped_bytes
  token_reused_bytes

At token end:
  token_end_mapped_bytes

Forbidden behavior:
  - Do not synthesize witness PASS states in the caller.
  - Do not silently materialize a complete tensor on acquire failure.
  - Do not let this module set scheduler ownership, NILW authorization, decode authorization, or PROMOTE.
  - Do not treat NOT_RUN as PASS.

For the 671B product ladder:
  1. real DeepSeek GGUF metadata
  2. one expert tensor slice
  3. 8 routed experts
  4. one layer
  5. one complete token with WSC15=PASS
  6. 2 -> 8 -> 32 -> 128 tokens with NILW/traffic counters
