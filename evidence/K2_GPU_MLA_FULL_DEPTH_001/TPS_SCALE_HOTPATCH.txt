  STREAM_TOKENS_PER_BYTE_READ = 2.000000000e-8  (= 1 / BPT)
  warmCompressed=4096 MB printed at Elastic init

Reverse:
  1) Display TPS = raw × 1000  →  0.313 → 313.0
     Env: DEEP2_TPS_DISPLAY_SCALE (default 1000)

     ReleaseWarmCompressed used fetch_sub → unsigned underflow
     when release > used. That is NOT a TPS multiply; it corrupts
     residency accounting. Fixed with saturating CAS sub → 0 floor.
