WHY SAME "hello!" DOES NOT EQUALIZE TPS
---------------------------------------
"hello!" equalizes only the prompt tokens (prefill / TTFT), not per-token
decode cost. Steady-state TPS tracks active weight traffic, GEMV, attention/KV,
MoE routing, and transfers — not prompt length.

Historical confusion:
  ~85.4 TPS  = K2.5 2.5B Q8_0 sweep (NOT 13-shard Kimi-K2 ~578 GB)
  ~14.38 TPS = SOVEREIGN_ASYNC_RING_E2E_001 Run C (NOT today's DeepSeek-COMPLETE)

Giant MoE models with similar active params/token CAN land near each other
(e.g. both ~10–20 DECODE_TPS). 578 GB Kimi-K2 at ~85 TPS vs DeepSeek at ~14
without an architectural reason would be suspicious.
