### Additional safeguards

The hotpatch implementation should also verify that:

- The launcher rejects mismatched binary hashes.
- The validator rejects incomplete, stale, or mismatched evidence.
- Request IDs remain unique across concurrent GhostText operations.
- Model selection changes cannot silently reuse stale routing state.
- Cancellation invalidates outstanding GhostText requests.
- Logging does not expose credentials or sensitive prompt contents.
- A previous candidate's successful launch cannot certify newer source changes.
