INTERPRETATION - stated, not assumed
  Before the fix, convOut + inner (the B channel) and convOut + inner +
  groupBC (the C channel) pointed PAST the end of ssmY and therefore read
  whatever heap bytes followed. Those reads returned values that happened to
  keep the scan finite, so generation "succeeded" on uninitialized memory.
  After the fix those channels read the genuinely computed conv1d output, and
  that output explodes to ~1e11.
  The buffer overflow was therefore MASKING a real numerical defect in the
  SSM conv path. The pre-fix VERDICT=PASS in run002 was not merely invalid
  because the process crashed; the generations themselves were reading out of
  bounds.
